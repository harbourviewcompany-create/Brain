# First 30-Day Revenue Test Queue

## Rule

The Brain should not test all opportunity lanes at once. Each day has one primary money lane, a defined source set, a quota, and a kill/scale decision.

## Week 1: prove paid interest

### Day 1: High-intent lead pack

Goal: sell or get serious interest for one lead pack.

Source classes:

- founder communities
- forums
- LinkedIn/manual capture
- review complaints
- public recommendation requests

Quota:

- 50 raw signals
- 20 logged
- 10 qualified
- 5 prioritized
- 3 outreach actions
- 1 sellable sample pack

Offer: $150 to $500 High-Intent Lead Pack.

Success: 3 replies or 1 paid buyer.

Kill/modify: fewer than 2 replies after 50 targeted messages.

### Day 2: Buyer/seller match sprint

Goal: find one asset, inventory, company, service, or demand signal and identify counterparties.

Source classes:

- marketplaces
- auction/liquidation sites
- business-for-sale listings
- industry directories
- public buyer requests

Offer: $500 to $2,500 Buyer/Seller Match Sprint plus success-fee path where appropriate.

Success: 2 counterparty conversations or 1 paid sourcing request.

### Day 3: Procurement/RFP match

Goal: find open buying requirements and match them to qualified vendors.

Source classes:

- government procurement portals
- municipal tenders
- hospital/university/school procurement
- nonprofit procurement
- corporate supplier portals

Offer: $250 to $1,500 Procurement / RFP Match Pack.

Success: 3 vendor replies or 1 paid brief.

### Day 4: Local business gap scan

Goal: find local businesses with visible gaps that agencies or service providers can monetize.

Source classes:

- Google Maps/manual review
- local business directories
- review platforms
- local permit/opening data
- websites with broken lead capture

Offer: 48-Hour Local Opportunity Scan.

Success: 3 agency/service-provider replies or 1 paid scan.

### Day 5: Hiring pain intelligence

Goal: use urgent hiring signals to identify operational pain, expansion, or service/vendor need.

Source classes:

- job boards
- company career pages
- founder posts
- LinkedIn/manual capture
- funding announcements as secondary context

Offer: Hiring-Pain Lead Pack or candidate/source map.

Success: 3 recruiter/agency/operator replies or 1 paid pack.

### Day 6: Distressed asset discovery

Goal: find asset/liquidity pressure and match to buyers.

Source classes:

- liquidation listings
- closing business posts
- business-for-sale sites
- used equipment marketplaces
- bankruptcy/insolvency/public notices where legally usable

Offer: Distressed Asset Buyer Match or Distress Signal Report.

Success: 2 buyer conversations or 1 paid alert/intro path.

### Day 7: review and reallocate

Goal: select scale/modify/kill decisions.

Actions:

- compare reply rates
- compare revenue
- compare operator hours
- compare legal/access risk
- update lane priority scores
- choose the next week's top 2 lanes

## Weeks 2-4: compound what works

### Week 2

Double down on the best two lanes from Week 1. Add source specificity: exact sites, exact search queries, exact communities, and exact buyer lists.

### Week 3

Turn the best lane into a repeatable productized offer with a sample deliverable, price, outreach script, follow-up script, and delivery checklist.

### Week 4

Automate only the proven pieces:

- source monitoring
- signal capture
- scoring
- offer packaging
- follow-up reminders
- outcome logging

Do not automate external outreach without approval gates.

## Weekly review fields

- lane tested
- signals reviewed
- opportunities qualified
- outreach sent
- replies
- meetings
- paid conversions
- revenue
- operator hours
- revenue per hour
- false positives
- legal/access issues
- best source
- worst source
- best script
- scale/modify/kill decision
- next automation candidate
