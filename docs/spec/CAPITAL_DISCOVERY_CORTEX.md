# Capital Discovery Cortex

## Status

Canonical scope extension for the Brain. This document preserves the economic-cognition and capital-discovery concepts developed in project doctrine. It does not replace the cognitive substrate; it defines the money-oriented cortex layered on top of it.

## Primary objective

The Brain's cognitive substrate exists to perceive, model, reason, learn, and act. The Capital Discovery Cortex adds an explicit economic objective: detect monetizable change, validate it, route it to controllable transactions, learn from realized economic outcomes, and compound the resulting capital and proprietary assets.

Canonical chain:

`WORLD MOVEMENT -> PERCEPTION -> CHANGE -> PRESSURE -> ASYMMETRY -> AFFORDANCE -> OPPORTUNITY -> MONEY PATH -> TRANSACTION -> REVENUE/PROFIT -> ATTRIBUTION -> LEARNING -> CAPITAL ALLOCATION -> COMPOUNDING`

The system must optimize risk-adjusted net economic value under capital, operator-attention, legal, trust, and execution constraints. Raw revenue alone is not sufficient.

## Non-goals and guardrails

- The cortex is not a guarantee of profit.
- The cortex must not classify inference as verified fact.
- External outreach, spending, transaction commitment, publication, legal claims, sensitive conclusions, and other consequential actions remain approval-gated.
- The cortex must not use stolen data, unauthorized account access, prohibited scraping, illegal market manipulation, deceptive impersonation, or unsafe handling of personal or regulated data.
- Global scope does not override source terms, privacy law, sanctions, licensing, brokerage rules, or jurisdiction-specific restrictions.

These are first-class `EconomicAsymmetry` objects. An opportunity may reference multiple asymmetries.

`BUY, SELL, BROKER, REFER, RECRUIT, SOURCE, VERIFY, PACKAGE, AUTOMATE, ADVISE, LICENSE, PUBLISH, INTRODUCE, FINANCE, AGGREGATE, ARBITRAGE, RESELL, MONITOR, NEGOTIATE, MATCH, BUILD, ACQUIRE, RENT, LEASE, LIQUIDATE, CONSOLIDATE, INSURE, CERTIFY, TRAIN, ROUTE, RANK, ALERT`

These verbs power the affordance engine. One observation may generate several candidate affordances.

## Transaction control

A discovered transaction must distinguish economic possibility from fee control. Later-stage canonical objects include:

- Mandate
- IntroductionRecord
- FeeAgreement
- ReferralAgreement
- ExclusivityRecord
- OriginationEvidence
- DealRoom
- Transaction

Before sensitive disclosure or external commitment, the cortex should evaluate whether fee protection, mandate, exclusivity, or other commercial controls are required. Legal enforceability is jurisdiction-specific and must not be assumed.

## Affordance engine

Every economically relevant object should be evaluated for what can be done with it. Examples:

- used equipment -> sell, broker, lease, finance, match, price, liquidate
- new facility permit -> recruit, source equipment, introduce vendors, advise, finance, monitor
- hiring spike -> recruit, infer expansion, identify software/vendor demand, map competitor movement

Affordances are hypotheses until validated.

## Commercial skeptic / kill engine

Qualified opportunities must survive hostile review. Canonical kill questions include:

- Is there an identifiable payer?
- Is there budget or economic capacity?
- Is there urgency?
- Is evidence current and independently sufficient?
- Is the decision-maker reachable?
- Is there a viable payment mechanism?
- Can economics be protected where necessary?
- Is required capital acceptable?
- Is time to cash acceptable?
- Is legal/reputation risk acceptable?
- Is the opportunity already obvious and competitively crowded?
- Does the expected net value justify operator attention?

Disposition classes:

`ACT_NOW, VERIFY_FIRST, WATCH, ARCHIVE, KILL, AUTOMATE, DELEGATE, BUILD_AS_ASSET`

## Anti-obviousness and timing

## Operator attention

Operator attention is a scarce resource alongside compute and capital. The cortex must support explicit operator constraints and suppress low-value output.

Target disposition behavior is not `show everything`; it is `retain everything with provenance, surface only what justifies attention`.

## Revenue and profit attribution

Canonical attribution chain:

`RevenueEvent -> Transaction -> Opportunity -> MoneyPath -> Pressure/Asymmetry -> Signal -> Observation -> Source -> Sensor`

Major source promotion, strategy promotion, graph rewiring, capital reallocation, and commercial-learning updates remain blocked when attribution confidence is insufficient.

The economic objective prefers net profit and compounding value over vanity revenue.

## Capital ladder

The cortex must support capital-stage evolution from low-capital service/information/relationship plays toward larger owned positions and infrastructure. Canonical `CapitalState` includes available, reserved, risk, operating, and reinvestment capital.

Stage examples are organizational, not mandatory thresholds:

- knowledge / introductions / services
- better data / automation
- paid acquisition / contractors
- inventory / transaction positions
- equity / acquisitions
- owned infrastructure / platforms

## Productization and business-model mutation

The cortex must preserve the following emergence path:

`Observation -> Signal -> Opportunity -> Transaction -> Repeated Transaction Pattern -> Offer -> Product -> Business Model -> Owned Platform -> Capital Asset`

Repeated problems plus repeatable solutions plus repeatable payers are candidates for productization. Repeated buyer/seller matching may justify a private liquidity network, marketplace, transaction layer, or data subscription, subject to validation and legal review.
