# Originality Engine

The Originality Engine exists to stop The Brain from producing generic AI idea lists.

V1 requirements:

- every original idea must use at least three distinct memory or signal references
- every idea must explain why most people would miss it
- every idea must include a fastest validation test
- every idea must include a kill condition
- generic list, dashboard-only and scraper-only outputs are rejected
- every idea remains approval_required until debate and agency gates are satisfied

Originality is produced through cross-domain signal fusion, contrarian inversion, latent buyer discovery, data packaging, timing arbitrage, failure recycling and marketplace-gap detection.
