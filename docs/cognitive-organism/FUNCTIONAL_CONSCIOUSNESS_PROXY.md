# Functional Consciousness Proxy

The Brain must not claim literal subjective consciousness. V1 defines a buildable proxy:

```text
A persistent global workspace where The Brain integrates perception, memory, goal pressure, uncertainty, internal conflict and action pressure into a current self-state that guides future behavior.
```

The proxy requires:

- self-state snapshots
- competing goals
- active conscious focus
- curiosity pressure
- risk pressure
- revenue pressure
- immune quarantine
- proposed actions
- development events

The cockpit must show what The Brain is focused on, why, what it doubts, what it is curious about, what it has quarantined and what it wants to do next.
